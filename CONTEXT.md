# Workplace English tutoring

The product helps intermediate English learners recall and adapt appropriate workplace expressions through spoken practice.

## Language

**Scenario**:
A familiar category of workplace conversation, such as hosting a meeting, casual talk, or a one-on-one.
_Avoid_: Course, lesson

**Communication purpose**:
What the learner wants to accomplish in a conversation, such as welcoming colleagues or asking for support.
_Avoid_: Topic, phrase

**Expression**:
A reusable piece of English wording that serves a communication purpose and can be adapted to the situation.
_Avoid_: Script, required answer

**Learner turn**:
A spoken answer by the learner to the tutor's current question or prompt within a practice exchange. The question and answer together form a Q&A pair; only the learner's answer counts as the learner turn.
_Avoid_: Tutor turn, Q&A pair, whole conversation

**Practice exchange**:
A short spoken roleplay using Q&A, in which the tutor opens a situation and asks follow-up questions based on the learner's answers.
_Avoid_: Single answer, session

**Practice session**:
One instance of practicing a selected communication purpose, including the main exchange, coaching, optional focused retries, and a takeaway.
_Avoid_: Practice exchange, account

**Coaching**:
Feedback in which the tutor discusses the learner's speech and how to improve it, distinct from speaking as a character in the roleplay.
_Avoid_: Roleplay reply

**Exchange assessment**:
Feedback on the wording used in a practice exchange across naturalness and workplace tone, with a separate score for each dimension that has sufficient evidence.
_Avoid_: Overall grade, proficiency level

**Focused retry**:
A short opportunity to apply the main coaching suggestion in a small variation of the situation just practiced.
_Avoid_: Full practice exchange, required repetition

**Recent sessions**:
The learner's unfinished and completed practice sessions still available in the same browser within the temporary retention window.
_Avoid_: Permanent history, saved-expression library
